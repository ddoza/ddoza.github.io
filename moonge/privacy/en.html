Puff Puff Pop
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how ddoza (“we”) handles information in the mobile game Puff Puff Pop (뭉게뭉게 대작전, the “App”).

1. Summary

  • The App has no accounts or sign-in, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that identifies you.
  • Game progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • The App uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, which players watch only if they choose to. Ads are requested as non-personalized and general-audience only.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is kept in the App’s local storage on your device so you can continue playing:

  • Game progress (stages reached, weapons unlocked, best score)
  • Settings (language, music and sound effects on/off)
  • A daily count of rewarded ads watched, used to limit how often ads are offered

This information is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Information processed for ads (Google AdMob)

When a player chooses “Watch an ad to continue” on the game over screen, Google AdMob shows an ad. To load and show ads, Google may process:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language settings)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data

We apply the following settings to every ad request to minimize the data processed:

  • Child-directed treatment (COPPA) and under-the-age-of-consent tags, which turn off personalized and interest-based ads.
  • Non-personalized ads only.
  • General audience (G) rated ads only.
  • On iOS, the App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • On Android, the App does not use the advertising ID permission.

For how Google uses this information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. To show ads, the information in Section 3 is processed by Google LLC (United States) and sent to Google’s servers over the internet while an ad is shown.

5. Retention and deletion

We do not store any personal information. Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child’s information has been processed, please contact us and we will promptly take appropriate action.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. Since we hold no personal information, you can remove all game data by uninstalling the App. Requests related to ads can also be made through Google’s privacy tools.
