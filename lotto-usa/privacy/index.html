Lotto USA
Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Data Company (“we”, “us”) operates the Lotto USA app. No account or sign-up is required, and we don't store information that identifies you on our servers.

1. Information we process

  • Saved numbers: tickets you save are stored only on your device. They aren't sent to our servers.
  • Dream descriptions: text you enter for dream numbers is processed on your device only. It isn't saved or sent anywhere.
  • Automatically collected information: when the app requests draw results, our web server logs the IP address, request time, and request path. This is used only for server security (blocking abuse) and troubleshooting.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. In serving ads, Google and its ad network partners may collect device information such as your advertising identifier. This is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy, not by us — see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).

3. Retention

  • Saved numbers: not held on our servers (device storage only)
  • Web server access logs: kept for 30 days, then automatically deleted

4. Third parties and processors

We don't sell or share personal information with third parties beyond what's described above, and we don't use outside processors. The app is hosted on servers provided by Vultr (Seoul, South Korea), and the advertising information described in Section 2 may be shared with Google.

5. Deletion

Web server access logs are deleted beyond recovery once the retention period passes.

6. Your choices

You can delete any ticket you saved at any time from the My Numbers tab. For questions about server access logs, contact us using the information below.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security

All network traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and the server is protected by a web application firewall against abnormal access.
