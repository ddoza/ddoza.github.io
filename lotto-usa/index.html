Lotto USA Support

Thanks for using Lotto USA.

Frequently asked questions

Q. The latest drawing isn't showing up.
A. Official results are usually available the morning after each drawing (US Eastern time). If they still don't appear, check your Wi-Fi or cellular connection and reopen the app. If it keeps happening, let us know at the email below.

Q. What happens when I run out of free picks?
A. After you use up the day's free picks, you can watch a short rewarded ad to generate more. Free picks reset every day at midnight.

Q. How do I delete numbers I saved?
A. You can delete them from the My Numbers tab. Everything is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Q. The text is too small.
A. You can change the text size in Settings.

Q. Do I need to enter any personal information?
A. No. There's no sign-up, and nothing you enter is sent to our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Q. Can I buy tickets in the app?
A. No. Lotto USA is for entertainment and reference only and doesn't sell tickets.
