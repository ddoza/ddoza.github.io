Splish Splash Sub Support

Thank you for playing Splish Splash Sub.

FAQ

Q. How do I move the submarine?
A. Drag anywhere on the screen and the submarine follows your finger. Bubbles fire automatically. After you unlock the special move, you can use the Twinkle Lantern every time you collect 100 pearls.

Q. Where is my progress saved?
A. Stages reached, unlocked weapons, best score, and settings are stored only on your device. If you delete the app or switch devices, your progress will not carry over.

Q. When can I watch an ad to continue?
A. After you calm the first boss, the option appears on the game over screen. You can use it once per run, at least 3 minutes apart, up to 5 times a day. Watching is always optional; you can also simply start again.

Q. I watched the whole ad but didn’t get to continue.
A. An unstable internet connection can interrupt the ad or keep the reward from being applied. Please check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email us with your device model and what happened.

Q. How do I change the language or sound?
A. Tap the 🌐 button on the title screen to choose from 9 languages. Music and sound effects can each be turned on or off with the settings button on the title screen.

Q. Are there in-app purchases?
A. No. The game is free, and the only ads are optional rewarded ads for continuing.
