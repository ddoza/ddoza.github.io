Sunny Farm Tycoon
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how ddoza (“we”) handles information in the mobile game Sunny Farm Tycoon (햇살 농장 타이쿤, the “App”).

1. Summary

  • The App has no accounts or sign-in, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that identifies you.
  • Game progress is stored on your device and is never sent to us. We do not run a game server.
  • The App uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, which you watch only if you choose to. Ads are requested as non-personalized and general-audience only.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is kept in the App’s local storage so you can continue playing:

  • Game progress (farm, crops, gold, research, and purchased equipment)
  • Settings (language, music, sound effects, and notification preferences)
  • A daily count of rewarded ads watched, used to limit how often ads are offered

We never receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or use “Restart the farm from scratch” in the App. If you have turned on your device’s backup service (such as Android or iCloud backup), your device may include this data in your own backup, under that service’s terms.

3. Notifications

Reminders, such as a crop being ready to harvest, are scheduled on your device. They do not use a server, and no information is sent to us. You can turn them off in the App or in your device settings.

4. Information processed for ads (Google AdMob)

To load and show ads, Google may process:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
  • Device identifiers, including the advertising ID on Android
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language settings)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data

Non-personalized ads are not based on your past behavior, but this information may still be used to limit how often an ad appears, prevent fraud and abuse, and produce aggregated ad reports.

We apply these settings to ad requests:

  • Non-personalized ads only.
  • General audience (G) rated ads only.
  • On iOS, the App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. For how Google uses this information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Your choices in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent message before requesting ads. The game works whatever you choose, though some ad-based rewards may be unavailable. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Notifications › Privacy › Ad Privacy Settings.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. To show ads, the information in Section 4 is processed by Google LLC (United States) and Google’s partners, and is sent over the internet to their servers.

7. Retention and deletion

We do not store any personal information. Game data is deleted as described in Section 2. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child’s information has been processed, please contact us and we will promptly take appropriate action.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Since we hold no personal information, you can remove all game data by uninstalling the App. Requests related to ads can also be made through Google’s privacy tools.

11. Changes to this policy
